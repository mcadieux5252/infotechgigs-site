Trusting your career to job boards and social sites just isn’t going to cut it.

Four ways InfoTechGigs keeps you ahead of your market.

Find

Individually matched roles, sent by text, from thousands of corporate job sites and hiring teams. Every role is scored against your stack, career level, certifications and work type, so you only hear about jobs that fit. No more sifting through listings and wasting valuable time.

Apply

How you apply depends on where the role came from.

Roles we find

We read job listings from thousands of corporate career sites every day to bring you the best options, then send you straight to the employer’s own application. No job board in the middle, no reposted listing, no third-party recruiters calling you. We take you directly to the source.

Roles employers post with us

Reply INTERESTED and we deliver your resume directly to the hiring team. These employers pay to reach InfoTechGigs’ private, opt-in membership through our matching engine, so your reply lands with someone who is actively hiring. No ghost listings, no wasted time.

Know

Critical market insights from weekly reports built around your needs, so you’re making the most informed decisions about where your career goes next.

  • Hiring trends: who’s hiring, at what level and for which skills, across cloud, security, data, software and infrastructure, drawn from thousands of job postings.
  • Industry trends: a curated read on the topics you follow, from cloud and AI shifts to security threats and new platforms, drawn from thousands of news outlets.

Benchmark

See what your peers see. Short member surveys on pay, equity, demand and career moves, shared back to every member so you can measure your own position: an annual salary and compensation survey, plus a quick question every week to keep your finger on the pulse.

Not looking? That’s the point.

The best time to understand your market is before you need a new role. Members who aren’t actively searching still get the weekly trends and peer benchmarks, moving from job searching to intelligent career management.

When the right opportunity shows up, you already know what it’s worth, who else is hiring and how you compare. Your next negotiation, internal or external, starts with data.
